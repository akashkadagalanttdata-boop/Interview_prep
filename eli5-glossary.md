# ELI5 Glossary

Your own plain-language textbook. Every entry is a concept you explained to a five-year-old
standard and passed (grade 2).

**By design, this is the artifact that survives everything else.** No AI needed to use it:
cover the explanation, say it aloud from the term alone, uncover, compare. If you can't
reproduce it, the concept goes back in `weak-spots.md`.

Target: ~250 entries by week 26.

## The bar (from `TUTOR.md` §7)

- **≤120 words.** One concrete physical analogy. No numbers-as-explanation.
- **Banned words:** the concept's own name and its technical siblings. *Attention* can't use
  query, key, value, weight, token, vector. *KV cache* can't use cache, store, reuse,
  recompute. *Consistent hashing* can't use hash, node, ring, rebalance.
- **Grade 2 required to appear here:** a child could repeat it back and be roughly right.
  Grade 1 (accurate but abstract) and grade 0 (smuggled jargon, or needs a follow-up question
  to make sense) don't make the cut.

If an entry here later fails a cold re-test, **demote it** — delete it, drop the concept to L3
in `progress.md`, and log it in `weak-spots.md`. An inflated glossary is worse than a short one.
